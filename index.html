About Temporary Email

What is a temporary email?

A temporary email is a disposable address used for short-lived communication, such as verification messages, while keeping your primary inbox separate.

Is it free?

Yes. The website interface is free to use. Availability and retention depend on the mail infrastructure behind the service.

Should I use it for important accounts?

No. Disposable addresses can expire and should not be used for banking, account recovery, identity verification, or anything you need to retain permanently.

Important: do not promise “100% anonymous”, “100% secure”, “unlimited”, or a fixed deletion period unless your backend actually guarantees those claims.
