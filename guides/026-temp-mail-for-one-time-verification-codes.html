Troubleshooting Delivery Delays and Domain Blocks

If a verification passcode does not appear within 10 seconds, the issue is typically caused by external factors rather than the disposable inbox itself:

  • Third-Party Outbound Queue Throttling: High-traffic web platforms often batch transactional emails in outbound delivery queues (such as SendGrid or Amazon SES) that can take 30 to 60 seconds during peak hours.
  • Strict Domain Blacklist Filtering: If a target platform detects that the domain belongs to a temporary mail service, its firewall may silently drop the outgoing email without displaying an error on screen.
  • Disconnected WebSocket Sessions: If your device loses internet connectivity or your browser suspends the background tab, the live feed may pause. Refreshing or switching to a new domain will re-establish a clean connection.
