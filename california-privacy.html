California Privacy Statement

This notice provides additional information for California residents about privacy rights that may apply to personal information processed through TempMail Box.

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This statement supplements our Privacy Policy. It is intended as a general privacy notice and should be read together with the actual data practices of the service.

Important: Privacy rights and obligations can depend on the service, the information involved, and applicable law. This page does not replace legal advice or a formal legal determination of which laws apply.

1. Categories of Information

Depending on how TempMail Box is used and how the infrastructure is configured, information processed may include:

  • Identifiers and contact-related information: temporary email addresses and information contained in messages.
  • Internet or network activity: IP address, browser/device information, request information, timestamps, and interactions with the website where processed.
  • Electronic communications: email messages, sender information, subjects, dates, and message content delivered to a temporary address.
  • Inferences or technical information: information needed to maintain security, detect abuse, troubleshoot problems, or improve reliability.

2. Business and Commercial Purposes

Information may be processed to provide the temporary email service, operate and secure the website, prevent abuse and fraud, troubleshoot technical problems, maintain infrastructure, measure performance, comply with legal obligations, and support analytics or advertising where enabled.

3. Sources of Information

Information may come directly from your browser or device, from messages sent to a temporary email address, from service and infrastructure providers, or from your interactions with the website.

4. Sale or Sharing

Do not interpret this page as a blanket statement that information is never sold or shared. Whether information is “sold,” “shared,” or otherwise disclosed depends on the actual vendors, advertising systems, analytics tools, and data flows configured for the service.

If advertising or analytics systems that constitute regulated sharing are enabled, the applicable disclosures and choices should be updated to accurately describe those systems.

5. Retention

Temporary email messages and addresses may be retained only as long as necessary under the configured service infrastructure. Technical and security information may have separate retention periods.

6. California Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable California privacy law and your circumstances, California residents may have rights concerning personal information, which can include rights to know about certain information collected, request deletion, correct certain information, opt out of certain forms of sale or sharing, and receive equal treatment for exercising applicable rights.

7. How to Submit a Request

To submit a privacy-related request, email pdfgridbox@gmail.com and clearly state that your message is a privacy request. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify the request and protect against unauthorized access.

8. Authorized Agents

Where applicable, a California resident may use an authorized agent to submit a request. Additional verification or authorization documentation may be required.

9. Sensitive Information

TempMail Box is not designed as a secure repository for highly sensitive information. Do not intentionally send passwords, financial credentials, government identification numbers, private keys, or other confidential information to a disposable address.

10. Contact
