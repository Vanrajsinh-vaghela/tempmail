Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use TempMail Box and how that information is used to operate the service.

Important: TempMail Box is a disposable email service. Do not use temporary inboxes for passwords, banking information, account recovery, confidential documents, or other sensitive information.

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This policy applies to the TempMail Box website and related temporary email functionality available at tempmail.pdfgridbox.in.

1. Information We Process

You generally do not need to create an account to use TempMail Box. Depending on how the service is used and configured, we may process information necessary to operate the temporary inbox.

  • Temporary email addresses generated by the service.
  • Email messages delivered to those temporary addresses, including sender, subject, date, and message content.
  • Technical information such as IP address, browser/device information, timestamps, request information, and security logs where processed by our hosting or infrastructure providers.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be processed to provide the temporary email service, display incoming messages, detect and prevent abuse, maintain security, troubleshoot technical problems, improve reliability, comply with legal obligations, and operate optional analytics or advertising where enabled.

3. Temporary Email Content

Messages sent to a temporary address may be processed by the mail-routing and storage infrastructure required to deliver and display them. Temporary inboxes are not intended to be permanent storage.

Because retention depends on the actual backend configuration, we do not promise a specific deletion time unless that retention rule is technically enforced by the service.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

TempMail Box may use essential browser storage or cookies for functionality and security. If analytics or advertising is enabled, additional technologies may be used according to the choices available through our Cookie Settings page.

5. Third-Party Providers

The service may rely on third-party infrastructure such as hosting, CDN, DNS, email-routing, database, analytics, security, or advertising providers. Those providers may process information as necessary to provide their services and according to their own policies.

6. Data Retention

Temporary email data may be retained only for as long as required by the configured service infrastructure. Technical and security logs may have separate retention periods. Actual retention should be determined by the backend and provider settings in use.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to operating an online service. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Do not send highly sensitive or confidential information to disposable addresses.

8. Children's Privacy

The service is not intended to encourage unlawful or harmful activity by minors. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a way that requires attention, contact us at the address below.

9. Your Privacy Requests

Where applicable under privacy law, you may contact us with questions or requests concerning personal information processed through the service. We may need to verify a request before taking action.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service, technology, legal requirements, or data practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
